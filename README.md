# 🥾 Triplo

**Your trip, multiplied by connections**

**Triplo** is a mobile application dedicated to trekking enthusiasts, developed in **Flutter** as a group project for Politecnico di Milano. The app combines outdoor exploration with social features and personal tracking, offering users a unique space to discover new trails and document their adventures.

## ✨ Key Features

* **🗺️ Interactive Map & Routes:** Explore an integrated map featuring pre-set trekking routes. By selecting a trail, you can view all the technical information and useful details for your hike.
* **🔖 Save Routes:** Save your favorite trails to a personal list to keep them handy and easily plan your next outings.
* **📖 Trekking Journal:** Create personalized journal pages for every completed adventure. Enrich your stories by saving photos of the hike, personal experiences, and challenges overcome along the way.
* **👥 Social & Sharing:** Follow your friends to discover their activities and tag them directly in your journal pages for shared experiences, keeping the memories of your group hikes alive.

## 🛠️ Main Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Flutter (Dart)
* **Backend & Cloud:** Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Authentication)
* **Integrations:** Interactive maps, location services, and GPS tracking
